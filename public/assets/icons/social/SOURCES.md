# Social icon sources

The files in this directory are local, self-contained SVGs. No image is loaded
from a third-party host at runtime.

- `vk.svg` — VK brand mark and colour (`#0077FF`), normalized from the
  [Simple Icons VK asset](https://simpleicons.org/?q=vk). Brand reference:
  [VK](https://vk.com/brand).
- `telegram.svg` — Telegram paper-plane mark and brand colour (`#26A5E4`).
  Telegram explicitly provides its logos for buttons and illustrations on its
  [official logo page](https://telegram.org/tour/screenshots).
- `taplink.svg` — compact Taplink mark: blue circle and three parallel rounded
  lines, redrawn as clean geometry for small UI sizes. Brand reference:
  [Taplink](https://taplink.cc/).

Retrieved/redrawn on 2026-08-28. VK, Telegram and Taplink names and marks are
trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to identify links to
the corresponding official ANESTET profiles.
