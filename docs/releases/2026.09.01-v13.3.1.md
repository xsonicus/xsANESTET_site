# ANESTET 2026.09.01-v13.3.1

Packshot quality and export-performance release.

## Approved change

- Products 17, 33, 34, 35, 36, 37, 38, 39 and 40 no longer reuse their visibly quantized 12-level alpha edges.
- Each packaging family has a fitted SVG silhouette: straight body walls, cap and base arcs, shoulder curves, pump components or dropper geometry.
- The rejected blur approach is not present. SVG is rendered at 4× and reduced with Lanczos solely for vector antialiasing.
- `CopyOpacity` replaces alpha only. Automated comparison proves zero changed RGB pixels, preserving every official label and product detail.
- The other fourteen already smooth product cutouts remain unchanged.

## Performance work

- The catalogue uses lossless 600×600 card variants while hero and detail views retain 1000×1000 lossless masters.
- Postbuild pruning removes unused legacy source folders and raw processing PNGs from `out/` only. The repository keeps all provenance masters.
- Current export pruning removes 35.34 MiB; the final deployable `out/` is 28 MiB.
