# ANESTET 2026.09.01-v13.3.0

Feature release for the first-screen shopping route, official product detail experience, stable Future Beauty composition and price typography.

## Approved changes

- The hero now ends with a compact white strip: «Купить сразу / или подобрать» and two direct actions.
- Product packshots and titles open a detail dialog. Content is derived from the authorized official MODX export; cart actions remain independent.
- The Opaline group and its halo share one fixed responsive anchor. Its vertical coordinate is the measured mean alpha centre of all 23 packshots: 49.7174% of their common canvas.
- Old and new hero prices no longer overlap during Prism. Open Sans semibold/bold faces and tabular numerals remove browser-synthesized mixed weight.
- Mobile copy height is reduced so the quick route fits within a normal first viewport.
