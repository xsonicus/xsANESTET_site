# ANESTET 2026.09.02-v13.4.4

## Scope

This patch corrects the remaining Retina edge artifact in large product detail cards, removes their cold-image blank state and tightens the care-system-to-guide transition requested in the latest visual review.

## Product media

- Every one of the 23 products has a dedicated transparent 2000×2000 WebP detail asset.
- IDs 17 and 33–40 are rasterized at the final 2× canvas directly from approved SVG silhouettes: one fitted top curve, straight walls and the approved bottom curve. No blur or generative reconstruction is used.
- The remaining 14 already-approved continuous masks are enlarged with alpha-aware Lanczos sampling; labels and product appearance are not regenerated.
- The complete detail set weighs approximately 1.6 MiB; individual files are 43–128 KiB and remain below the 320 KiB release budget.

## Stable opening

- Product image/title interactions preload the matching detail asset on pointer intent, keyboard focus or touch intent.
- The cached 600×600 catalog derivative is visible on the first dialog frame and remains until the 2000×2000 image has decoded.
- A 160 ms opacity handoff changes only the image source layer and never the card geometry.
- Automated interaction QA delays the Retina request by 500 ms, verifies a visible cached preview during the delay, then verifies a complete 2000×2000 replacement.

## Care-system density

- `Выбор по этапу, не по догадке` is replaced by the approved `Выбор по этапу`.
- The lower care-system space is capped at 64 px on desktop and 42 px on mobile.
- The following guide starts within 68 px on desktop / 48 px on mobile, and its route line begins within 56 px.
